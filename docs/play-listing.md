## Full description (max 4000)

Pay the shops you visit every day without scanning their QR code every time.

Scan a merchant's UPI QR code once and pin it to your home screen. From then on, tapping the icon opens your UPI app straight on that merchant's payment screen. Just enter the amount and pay.

HOW IT WORKS
• Scan the shop's QR code with the camera, or load it from a photo or screenshot
• Pin it to your home screen as an icon with the shop's initial and a colour you choose
• Tap the icon to open your UPI app on the payment screen for that shop

FEATURES
• Add merchants by scanning, from an image, by sharing a QR image or UPI link from another app, or by typing the link
• Choose which UPI app to use for each merchant, or use your phone's default
• Edit a merchant and its home-screen icon updates too
• Long-press the app icon for your most-used merchants
• Home-screen widget with all your merchants
• Export and import your merchants as a file
• Warnings for QR codes that look risky to save: a fixed amount, a one-time payment reference, a personal UPI ID rather than a shop, or a duplicate

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• The app has no internet access, so your saved merchants never leave your phone
• QR scanning is done on your phone by Google Play services

GOOD TO KNOW
• Payments are made in your own UPI app. This app only opens it with the saved payment details, and never sees your bank account, PIN or payment result.
• Shops sometimes change their QR code. If the code at the counter changes, scan it again.
• Android doesn't let apps remove home-screen icons. When you delete a merchant, its icon is disabled and you remove it yourself.

UPI Shortcuts is an independent app. It is not affiliated with NPCI, any bank or any UPI app.
