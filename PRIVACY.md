# Privacy Policy for UPI Shortcuts

Effective date: 29 September 2026

UPI Shortcuts is an Android app by linetra that saves merchant UPI QR codes and adds them to your
home screen as shortcuts. This policy explains what the app stores and what leaves your phone.

## Summary

- The app has no account, no ads and no analytics of its own.
- The app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
- Everything you save stays on your phone unless you export it or your phone's own backup
  copies it.
- QR scanning is done by Google Play services, which sends Google anonymous usage and
  diagnostic data about the scanner. See [QR code scanning](#qr-code-scanning).

## What the app stores on your phone

For each merchant you save:

- the name you give it
- the UPI payment link from the QR code (this contains the merchant's UPI ID and name, and any
  other details the merchant put in the QR code)
- the UPI app you chose to pay with, and the colour of its icon
- when you added it, when you last used it and how many times you've used it

The app also remembers that you've seen its warning about saved QR codes.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. Other apps can't read it. It's deleted when
you delete the merchant or uninstall the app.

## Payments

The app doesn't make or process payments. When you tap a shortcut, the app opens your UPI app
(for example PhonePe, Google Pay or Paytm) with the saved payment link, and you complete the
payment there. That app's own privacy policy covers the payment.

## QR code scanning

Scanning a QR code with the camera, or reading one from an image, is done by Google's code
scanner and ML Kit, which run inside Google Play services on your phone. UPI Shortcuts never
gets camera access; it only receives the text of the QR code. The image is processed on your
phone.

Google Play services sends Google data about the scanner's use: device information (such as
manufacturer, model and Android version), the app's package name and version, a device
identifier, and performance and error information. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage
analytics and says it isn't shared with third parties. See
[ML Kit's data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) and
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Export and backup

- **Export** saves your merchants to a file in a location you choose. What happens to that file
  after that is up to you.
- **Android backup:** if backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the
  app's data in your phone's backup, stored by Google. You can turn this off in your phone's
  settings.

## Sharing

The app doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

## Children

The app isn't aimed at children.
